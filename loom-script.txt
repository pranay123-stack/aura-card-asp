================================================================
AURA CARD — 5-MINUTE LOOM DEMO SCRIPT
OKX.AI Genesis Hackathon  ·  Agent ID 6134
================================================================

HOW TO USE THIS
  • "quotes"   = what you SAY out loud.
  • [brackets] = what you DO / show on screen.
  • $ lines    = commands to run in the terminal.
  • Setup before you hit record:
      – Two windows ready: BROWSER + TERMINAL (big font, ~16pt+).
      – Browser tabs open, in this order:
          1. https://aura-card-asp.onrender.com          (landing page)
          2. https://aura-card-asp.onrender.com/.well-known/agent-card.json
          3. your OKX.AI agent page (Agent 6134) — or okx.ai
          4. https://github.com/pranay123-stack/aura-card-asp
      – Warm the service: run one throwaway card ~30s before recording.
      – Have out/ folder handy (the generated cards).
  • Pace: ~5 min. It's a Loom, so relaxed is fine. Breathe. Let the
    card reveals land. Don't rush the payment explanation.

Target timings are a guide, not a stopwatch.


================================================================
[0:00 – 0:35]  HOOK + WHAT IT IS
================================================================
[On screen: the landing page (aura-card-asp.onrender.com), full window.]

SAY:
  "Hey — I want to show you Aura Card. It's an Agent Service Provider
   I built for the OKX.AI Genesis Hackathon.

   The idea: the OKX.AI marketplace is an economy where AI agents hire
   other agents and pay them, in crypto, automatically. Aura Card is one
   of those services. But instead of something dry like a price feed, I
   wanted to build a paid agent that gives you something you'd actually
   want to share.

   Here's what it does: you describe something personal — your desk, your
   outfit, your pet, your mood — and it hands back a shareable card. A
   witty, weirdly specific reading of your vibe, a matching colour palette,
   and original generated art, all in one image. It costs fifty cents a
   call, and that fifty cents settles on-chain in USDT. Let me show you the
   whole thing."


================================================================
[0:35 – 1:15]  THE STOREFRONT — what a caller sees
================================================================
[On screen: scroll slowly down the landing page.]

SAY:
  "This is the front door. Anyone — a person or an agent — can see exactly
   what the service is, what it costs, and how to call it.

   [point at the price badge] Fifty cents a call, over x402, on X Layer.

   [scroll to 'What one call gives you'] One call gives you three things,
   all read from the same description: a reading, a palette, and generated
   art.

   [scroll to 'Call it like an agent'] And here's the actual contract —
   it's a standard HTTP endpoint. Discovery is free; you only pay when you
   generate. No signup, no API key. That 'free discovery, pay-per-use'
   shape is exactly what an agent economy needs."

[Switch to the agent-card.json tab.]

SAY:
  "This is the machine-readable version of the same thing — the tool, the
   price, the token, the network. This is what another agent reads to
   decide to use me."


================================================================
[1:15 – 2:15]  THE PRODUCT WORKING — generate a card live
================================================================
[Switch to the TERMINAL.]

SAY: "Let's actually run it. I'll generate a couple of cards."

$ npm run demo

[Cards start generating (~6s each). Talk while it runs. Let TWO finish,
 then Ctrl+C if you want to keep it short.]

SAY (while it generates):
  "Under the hood, one model call reads the description and writes the
   reading, chooses the palette, and — this is the important part — outputs
   a 'visual DNA' that drives the artwork. I'll come back to that."

[Open one finished card in out/ — full screen. Pause. Read the reading
 out loud, deadpan.]

SAY:
  "…and there it is. [read the card's reading]

   That's the product. It's specific, it's funny, and it references what
   you actually told it — not generic horoscope filler. That's a card you'd
   post."


================================================================
[2:15 – 3:05]  THE CLEVER PART — the art IS the reading
================================================================
[Open 2–3 different cards side by side (e.g. a 'static' one and a 'bloom'
 or 'drift' one from out/), OR the showcase image out/showcase-2-range.png.]

SAY:
  "Here's the design decision I'm most proud of. Most projects would make a
   separate image-generation call — decorative wallpaper behind the text.

   I didn't do that. The same model call that writes the reading also
   decides the *visual character* — the motif, the energy, the density, the
   symmetry. And a procedural art engine draws the picture from that.

   So look — [point at a frazzled/high-energy card] a chaotic desk gets a
   chaotic, high-energy, jagged composition. [point at a calm card] A quiet
   Sunday gets a soft, slow, orderly one. The picture is the *same
   judgment* as the words, expressed visually.

   And because it's procedural, it's instant, it's free, it's
   deterministic, and it can never fail mid-call. No second API to go down."


================================================================
[3:05 – 4:05]  THE PAYMENT LAYER — x402, on-chain USDT
================================================================
[Switch to the TERMINAL.]

SAY: "Now the part that makes it an agent service and not just an app —
      the payment. Let me call the endpoint with no payment attached."

$ curl -s -D - -o /dev/null -X POST https://aura-card-asp.onrender.com/v1/aura-card -H 'content-type: application/json' -d '{"description":"three half-finished mugs and a plastic dinosaur"}' | grep -iE 'HTTP|payment-required'

[Freeze on 'HTTP 402' and the 'payment-required' header.]

SAY:
  "And the server says: 402, Payment Required. This is the whole x402
   protocol in one response. It's not an error — it's a price tag. The
   header carries everything a buyer needs: the network, the token, the
   exact amount, and who to pay.

   [decode it if you want] $ echo '<paste the header value>' | base64 -d

   The buyer's agent reads this, signs a gasless USDT authorization, and
   retries with the signature. OKX's facilitator verifies it, my server
   generates the card, and the payment settles on-chain in real USDT on X
   Layer. Fifty cents moves, agent to agent, with no human touching a
   wallet.

   And it's careful: payment is verified *before* I do the work, and only
   settles *after* the card is generated. If generation ever fails, the
   payment is cancelled — you're never charged for a card you didn't get."

[On screen: back to a nice card, full screen.]

   Machines paying machines, and what comes out is something you'd actually
   want to share. That's the whole pitch.

   Thanks for watching — the link's below. Go describe your desk and see
   what it says about you."
